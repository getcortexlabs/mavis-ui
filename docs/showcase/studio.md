# Atelier

An editorial reading experience with serif headings and a separate body family. Switch between daytime and night editions: the quote card retains its nested theme and the surrounding catalog header stays independent.

<a href="/playground/#/showcase/studio" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open Atelier ↗</a>

Open the edition, enter an email and confirm the subscription to explore Sheet, Dialog and Toast in the local theme. The Portuguese content is a demonstration: no email is sent, registered or persisted. Unmounting the screen clears its data.
